Privacy Policy for WebReader

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

San Tran ("we", "our", or "us") provides the WebReader application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We collect limited information automatically to provide the service, fund the free version, and improve app stability:

  • Device or other identifiers: Google AdMob and Firebase may collect advertising, app-set, installation, or similar identifiers for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and app functionality.
  • Approximate location: Google AdMob may use the device IP address to estimate a general location.
  • App interactions: Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase may collect app launches, taps, ad interactions, sessions, and playback-related events.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob may collect stack traces, relevant app state, device model, operating-system version, launch time, and other performance information.
  • Local data: Your library, reading history, URLs, reading position, and settings remain on your device. We do not sync this content to our servers or analytics services.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play services, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics. These providers process data according to their own privacy terms. Data transmitted by these services is encrypted in transit.

3. App Permissions

  • Internet: Used to load articles from URLs you provide.
  • Media Playback: Used to facilitate Text-to-Speech audio output.
  • Notifications: Used for playback control in the notification drawer.

4. Data Purpose & Safety

We do not sell your personal data. Google service providers process the limited data described above to provide ads, analytics, crash reporting, fraud prevention, security, and app functionality.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Advertising, analytical, and diagnostic data is retained according to the applicable Google service policies. Local app data is deleted when you uninstall the application. You may contact us to request deletion of data associated with the app where applicable.

6. Contact Us

If you have privacy questions or a data deletion request, contact us at admin@tripslay.com.
