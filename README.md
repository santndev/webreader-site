# WebReader — Web Page Reader for Android

Turn web articles into audio. Share a page link with WebReader or paste its URL, then listen while you commute, work, or take a screen break.

- Read article pages aloud with text-to-speech voices available on your Android device.
- Keep listening in the background with playback controls in your notifications and on the lock screen.
- Adjust playback speed and revisit your listening history.
- English and Vietnamese interface.
